    PDFs and files on your computer

    To read PDFs and text files opened from your computer (addresses starting with file://), Chrome needs your permission:

    1. Open chrome://extensions (paste it into the address bar).
    2. Find Voix and click Details.
    3. Turn on Allow access to file URLs, then reload the file's tab.

    What Voix can read

    Articles and web pages (including pages built with web components and documents inside frames), PDFs (opened in Chrome, embedded in pages, or saved on your computer; scanned pages are read with text recognition), Google Docs, Slides and Sheets, and plain-text files. For anything drawn as an image or canvas, "Read the whole page" recognises the text that is on screen.

    Privacy

    To turn articles into natural speech, Voix sends the article text (only the sentences being read) to the voice service configured above. The text is used to generate audio and is not stored by the Voix server.

    Voix never sends text from email and banking sites, and it only reads a page when you press Listen, Play, the toolbar icon or the keyboard shortcut (Alt+Shift+V by default; change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts).

    Your settings (voice, speed, toggles, widget position) are stored in this browser only.
