Voix privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Voix is a Chrome extension that reads web pages and documents aloud. It is published by Ebenezer Igbinoba ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data Voix handles and why.

What Voix sends, and when

Voix only sends text when you ask it to read: when you press Listen or Play, click the Voix icon, or use the keyboard shortcut. It then sends the sentences being read to the Voix voice service, which turns them into speech and returns the audio.

  • The text is used only to generate the audio. It is not stored, sold, shared or used for any other purpose.
  • The voice service keeps short-lived technical logs for security and reliability: the time of a request, the address of the device making it, and which part of the service was used. These logs never contain the text being read.
  • Pages such as email and online banking are never sent, even if you ask Voix to read them.

What stays on your computer

  • Finding the article on a page, extracting text from PDFs, and recognising text in scanned documents or on screen all happen inside your browser.
  • Your settings (voice, speed, highlight and auto-scroll switches, the reader's position, and sites where you turned off the reading offer) are stored in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • For Google Docs, Slides and Sheets, Voix downloads the document's text from Google using your existing Google session, in the same way your browser would when you choose to download the file. That text is handled exactly like any other text Voix reads.

What Voix does not do

  • No collection of your browsing history.
  • No sale or transfer of data to third parties. The voice service runs on servers rented from a hosting provider, which processes the requests only to run the service on our behalf.

Children

Voix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
